## Overview

A1 Solution is an inventory and sales management system for small businesses, built as a decoupled Next.js client and Express 5 API. The backend owns stock, suppliers, customers, sales, and billing logic.

## Backend architecture

- Express 5 and TypeScript with modular controllers, routes, and middleware
- MongoDB persistence with transaction support for stock and invoice consistency
- FIFO batch stock tracking and movement history
- Invoice lifecycle with draft and confirmed states
- Zod validation at request boundaries
- JWT authentication stored in HTTP-only cookies
- Centralized API response and error envelopes

## Scope

- Product catalogue with categories, brands, and units
- Supplier and customer records
- Stock intake, FIFO movement, and low-stock visibility
- Sales invoices, payments, and cancellation
- Dashboard and report aggregates

## Stack

Next.js, TypeScript, Express 5, MongoDB, Zod, and JWT.
