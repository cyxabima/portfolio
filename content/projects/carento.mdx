## Overview

Carento is a multi-vendor car rental platform with an async FastAPI backend and a React client. Vendors manage vehicle listings and bookings; customers search vehicles, reserve them, leave reviews, and maintain a wallet balance.

## Backend architecture

- FastAPI with separate route, service, and model layers
- Async PostgreSQL access through SQLModel
- Custom JWT authentication and authorization without an external auth service
- Role-based access control for Vendor and Customer scopes
- Versioned REST endpoints under `/api/v1`
- Booking, review, and wallet transaction domains

## Scope

- Vehicle CRUD scoped to the owning vendor
- Customer and vendor registration, login, and profile management
- Booking history and vendor-side reservation views
- Authenticated vehicle reviews
- Wallet balance and transaction history

## Stack

Python, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, SQLModel, React, and ShadCN UI.
