## Overview

The payload was built for the International Space Science and Scientific Payload
Competition (ISSSP) Track 2. It collects power and environmental telemetry from
an omnidirectional solar assembly and records the data locally for later
analysis.

## Implementation

- ESP32-S3 firmware written in C/C++
- Multi-axis current and voltage measurement through INA226 sensors
- I2C sensor bus with SPI-based storage
- Thermal monitoring across the payload
- Deterministic SD card logging with fixed record layout and flush boundaries

## Result

Track 2 Finalist, International Space Science and Scientific Payload Competition
(ISSSP 2026).
